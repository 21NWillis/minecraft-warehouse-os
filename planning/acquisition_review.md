## 2. DOCTRINE CONFLICTS — board rulings

**MEMO 1 — RFTools Builder vs. turtle fleet supremacy / the Surrendering. Ruling: CONTAIN.**
The Builder may dig. It may never build. Quarry cards: legal, in the Showroom or JAMD only — extraction is labor, and labor was already outsourced to machines when the Digital Miner was greenlit. Shape cards on campus: banned forever. Construction is ceremony — F8 or the Payroll or it didn't happen; **you cannot delegate liturgy.** The fleet is not a throughput tool; it is the Payroll, and the Corporation does not lay off the Payroll because a faster chest with ambition arrived. Practical seal: the Builder has no park file, no `builder.resume`, no anchorPose. It cannot survive a chunk unload or an amnesiac session. No resume, no résumé.

**MEMO 2 — Draconic Evolution vs. the Dyson curve as power endgame. Ruling: SPLIT.**
*Energy Core:* ADOPT. Storage is not generation; the Core is the Battery's battery and its sphericity is doctrinally harmonious. *Draconic Reactor:* BAN as generation, CONTAIN as chemistry. The law is already written — "fission proceeds ONLY as chemistry" — and the reactor inherits it: **generation is daylight or it is a rounding error.** Additionally: this reactor is the one machine in the pack with a literal meltdown mechanic. The House already operates a casino; the Corporation does not need a second one that pays out in craters. If chaos shards are required, the reactor runs contained, in the Showroom or a blast annex, unautomated, Operator-attended — the Rule of the Hand extends to any GUI block with *opinions*.

**MEMO 3 — RFTools Control vs. ComputerCraft. Ruling: CONTAIN (to the Museum).**
The Corporation speaks Lua. One RFTools Control unit may be kept in the Museum of Hard-Won Laws, unpowered, labeled "the dialect we declined." Its processor may never join the network. RFTools Power generators: no conflict; interim power was already an ethylene-and-solar ladder.
